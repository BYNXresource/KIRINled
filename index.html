Splits the strip and alternates colors at high frequencies for urgent visual alerts (e.g., Target Locked).

Pixel-Perfect Control

Direct memory access to address any individual LED index with a specific HEX color and brightness.

像素级单点控制：直接寻址任意一颗物理灯珠并指定其颜色和亮度，适合自定义极客玩法。

Trigger exact frequencies (Hz) and durations (ms) on the onboard buzzer concurrently with light effects.
